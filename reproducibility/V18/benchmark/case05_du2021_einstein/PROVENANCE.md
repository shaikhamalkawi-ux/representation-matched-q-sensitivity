# Case 5 provenance — Du 2021

Primary source: Wen Sheng Du, “A further investigation on q-rung orthopair fuzzy Einstein aggregation operators”, *Journal of Intelligent & Fuzzy Systems* 41 (2021) 6655–6673. DOI 10.3233/JIFS-210548.

The executable endpoint uses the four-alternative × four-criterion Table 1 matrix, criterion weight vector (0.145, 0.2, 0.355, 0.3), q-ROFEWA equations, the q=3 worked example, and Table 2 q-sensitivity values at q=4,6,8,10,15,20.

All inputs are admissible from q=2. The q=3 baseline and every Table 2 aggregate are independently reproduced to printed precision. The controlled path uses q0=3.
