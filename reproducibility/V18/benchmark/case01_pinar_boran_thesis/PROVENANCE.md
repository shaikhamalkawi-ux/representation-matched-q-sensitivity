# Case 1 provenance — Pınar 2020 thesis

Primary source: Adem Pınar, PhD thesis, Gazi University, June 2020,
“Q Seviyeli Bulanık Küme Temelli Tedarikçi Performans Değerlendirmesi”.

The executable transcription uses:
- Table 7.1: linguistic q-ROF scale and decision-maker expertise scale.
- Table 7.2a: 5 decision makers × 10 suppliers × 15 criteria = 750 linguistic ratings.
- Table 7.3: five expert criterion-importance ratings and published criterion weights.
- Eqs. 6.1–6.12: q-ROF TOPSIS pipeline.
- Table 7.4: separation measures at q=3, p=1.
- Table 7.5: closeness coefficients and baseline ranking.
- Figure 7.4(a): p=1 parameter analysis at q=2,...,10.

The only cost criterion in the supplier TOPSIS case is X3 (price); the thesis later explicitly
describes criterion 3 as the cost criterion when converting it to a benefit criterion for the
comparison methods.

No unpublished values are inferred. The raw Table 7.2a linguistic entries and Table 7.3
criterion ratings are encoded directly in `case_data.json`.
