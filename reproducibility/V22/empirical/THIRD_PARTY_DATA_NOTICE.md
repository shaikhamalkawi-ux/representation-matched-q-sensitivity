# Original code and third-party-derived data

The repository's CC0 dedication applies to original research program code and
author-created material only to the extent the authors hold those rights. It does
not waive source agencies' data rights or restrictions. No agency endorses this
research, its encodings, scores or decisions. Numerical arrays/inputs are
adaptations and must retain this notice, `SOURCE_CITATIONS.md`, and provenance.

- `data/qol_*.json`, QOL expected values and exclusions derive from European
  Commission DG REGIO data. Attribution: European Commission, DG REGIO, Quality
  of Life in European Cities 2023 (revised September 2024 workbook). Changes:
  selected criteria, share closure and Fraction orthopair encoding, followed by
  author model outputs. Source default CC BY 4.0 applies; retain attribution,
  source link and identification of modifications.
- `data/pisa_*.json`, PISA expected values/exclusions derive from OECD PISA 2022,
  Results Volume I Tables I.B1.3.1/2. Retain OECD attribution and identification of
  changes (aggregation of proficiency tails and cohort selection). OECD data
  terms permit adaptation/distribution with attribution; required acknowledgement
  must accompany onward redistribution and sublicensing. They are not our CC0.
- `data/caa_primary.json`, CAA expected arrays and exclusions derive from UK Civil
  Aviation Authority 2025 punctuality statistics. Cite UK CAA and retain source
  links and these modifications (direction aggregation, eligibility exclusions,
  orthopair encoding and model outputs). **CAA prohibits onward sale of its
  statistical data.** Do not describe this data as CC0, unrestricted resale or
  blanket Open Government Licence. This is a no-charge research adaptation.
- CMS HCAHPS, EPA AQI, and US Census ACS derived tables retain US government
  source attributions and methodological qualifications. No agency logos,
  endorsement, personal survey records or proprietary contractor software are
  included. Their inclusion is not a waiver of any third-party rights in material
  at a source website. No unrestricted rights claim is made for such material.

For authoritative and current terms consult the cited agencies' source/legal
pages. The release review records the terms checked when this module was curated.
This notice preserves source restrictions rather than offering legal advice.
