# Scientific scope and retained qualifications

Six purpose-selected official-data domains yield eleven related tables, not eleven
independent studies or prevalence evidence. The analyst-selected coordinates are
encodings of observed aggregate summaries, not native fuzzy elicitation, official
agency rankings, ground-truth utilities or estimates of sampling uncertainty.
They have varying nonmembership values and do not validate the manuscript's
two-criterion common-nonmembership sharp theorem.

The base rung is r=1. The common analyst-selected sensitivity grid is
q=1+k/64, k=0,...,960; source agencies specify no q. Representation-matched raw
grades are mu^(1/q), nu^(1/q). Criterion factors are one plus the column mean
of x log(x)+y log(y), using natural logarithms and 0 log(0)=0. Normalize those
factors across criteria. All three methods share these weights; this is a
controlled operator comparison, not a claim to reproduce every native published
weighting pipeline. Dynamic weights are recomputed; frozen-q1 and equal weights
give algebraically invariant matched scores, not 961 independent trials.

The algebraic WA and WG scores use membership-power minus nonmembership-power;
the accuracy tie-break from their source is not applied. Cosine TOPSIS uses fixed
ideals (1,0),(0,1), similarities (not distances), and plus/(plus+minus). No
closeness score is compared numerically to a WA/WG gap as though on the same scale.
The tolerance 1e-12 defines computational near ties only, not practical utility
resolution, a hypothesis test or a calibrated measurement threshold.

All 33 dynamic sampled top-set paths are constant. This negative outcome is
retained. Internal ranks can change; EPA changes its top set between operators,
which is not a q-induced switch. No method superiority, prediction accuracy,
clinical, causal, policy or statistical conclusion follows.

R15 comparison was designed after earlier WA outcomes were available: no held-out
claim is made. Historical WA pair maxima use all 961 nodes except CMS/EPA, which
use q=1,2,3,4,8,16. R15 pair comparisons use seven nodes q=1,2,3,4,8,12,16;
the wrapper keeps these diagnostics separate. A sampled negative is not a
continuum certificate. PISA's exact coordinate dominance and EPA WA boundary
saturation give only their stated elementary fixed-table all-finite-q controls.
QOL's interval certificate is a separate directed-arithmetic calculation.

The housing illustration was selected **after** observing Wyoming 22-to-15 and
North Dakota's sampled winning path. The figure and its all-rival gaps explain
that existing result. Seven adjacent-node sign brackets are not exhaustive root
isolation. Decimal60 is ordinary high precision, not outward rounding. Source
rounding and point estimates are not calibrated uncertainty boxes. Changing the
housing denominator is a table change, not a rung-induced reversal.

Selection failures are preserved. In particular CAA's four source closure failures
exclude two whole airports, not selected rows. An extra nine-bin structural gate
was added after the first model run and before the fresh unchanged replay; it must
not be relabeled wholly prospective. CMS missing/suppressed values are not zero;
the strict cohort drops boundary grades. PISA exclusions retain warning flags.

The published WG example has an unresolved discrepancy: first score computed
about 0.1305247788 versus printed 0.1701, yielding a different winner under the
stated reading. The cited 2026 correction concerns indeterminacy, not that score
table. The cosine example's 15 values are compatible with its six-decimal display.
The 29 source-example comparisons yielded 28 compatible and one incompatible
under analyst last-digit conventions, not authenticated rounding intervals.
The separate verify_published_examples.py reruns their literal numeric inputs and compares the original result; the eleven-table grid scripts do not perform this source audit or authenticate source documents.
An earlier independent comparator checker had a label-schema failure, later
corrected without changing formulas or inputs; success is not a claim that no
failed check ever occurred.

This package does not rerun official-source download/extraction, historical
literature acquisition, all old proof scripts, or specialist/author peer review.
It supplies exact derived inputs and source fingerprints for the stated replays.
