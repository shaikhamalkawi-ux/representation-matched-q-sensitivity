## Description
Reproducibility archive accompanying the manuscript “Representation-Matched q-Sensitivity: Rank Reversal and Decision Robustness in q-Rung Orthopair Fuzzy Models.” The archive contains the executable seven-case q-rung orthopair fuzzy benchmark, Qin stagewise and local sensitivity analysis, winner-reversal radius verification, He structural-control analysis, derived numerical outputs, and provenance records. The exact global Qin winner-reversal radius is not claimed; the verified bound is 0.000995 ≤ ρ_win ≤ 0.003514796840392034.
