## Description
Public reproducibility archive supporting the manuscript “Representation-Matched q-Sensitivity: Rank Reversal and Decision Robustness in q-Rung Orthopair Fuzzy Models.” The archive contains seven executable benchmark reconstructions, Qin/He verification code, the retained radius-certificate trace, directed-rounding verification, derived numeric records, and provenance files. Scientific claims remain bounded exactly as in the manuscript: the exact global winner-reversal radius is unknown and is not claimed.
